How a turn plays

You and your rival take turns, one day at a time. On yours, every unit gets one order.

  1. {{ m::unit(faction="western", asset="recon", label="Outrider") }}

    Move

    Each unit moves once, as far as its legs, wheels or wings and the terrain allow.

  2. {{ m::unit(faction="western", asset="siege", label="Catapult") }}

    Attack

    Unit matchups, health and terrain cover decide the damage. Survivors strike back.

  3. {{ m::unit(faction="western", asset="scout", label="Militia") }}

    Capture

    Foot soldiers take outposts and workshops. Every property pays funds each day.

  4. {{ m::unit(faction="western", asset="ironclad", label="Charger") }}

    Build

    Spend those funds at workshops, docks and roosts for fresh units.

  5. {{ m::unit(faction="southern", asset="scout", label="Enemy militia") }}

    End turn

    Then the enemy commander moves. Take their headquarters, or rout their army, to win.

Classic tactics, woodland scale

  • Capture outposts and storehouses for funds, then build at workshops, docks and roosts.
  • Twenty units on land, in the air and on the water: militia, cavalry, catapults, hawk riders, rafts and more.
  • Terrain matters: thickets and boulders shelter, creeks slow, roads speed you along.
  • Fog of war, weather, supply and stamina, just as the old campaigns taught us.
  • A map editor to paint your own battlefield.

Played Advance Wars?

Then you already know how to play

Paw & Claw follows the rules of Advance Wars 2 and Dual Strike, move for move. The units, terrain and commanders wear fur and feathers, but the battles play the way you remember.

The rules of the woods

  • The same damage formula, counterattacks and terrain stars.
  • 20-point captures, daily income, repairs and resupply on your own properties.
  • Indirect units that can't move and fire; fliers and boats that burn stamina every day.
  • Commanders modelled on the AW2 and Dual Strike COs, each with a CO Power and a Super CO Power.
  • Fog of war, weather and the classic island maps.

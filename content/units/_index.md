Every army fields the same twenty units in its own colours; the [commanders](/commanders/) are what
set armies apart. Each unit copies a unit from *Advance Wars 2* with the same stats, so the
classic names are listed beside the woodland ones.

Ground units are built at **workshops**, boats at **docks** and fliers at **roosts**. Foot
soldiers are the only units that can capture. Ranged (indirect) units can't move and fire in
the same turn, but nothing they hit can strike back.

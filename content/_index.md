## Command the woodland

**Paw & Claw** is a turn-based tactics game in the spirit of *Advance Wars*. Pick an army, pick a
commander, and fight for a map of meadows, brambles, creeks and ponds one move at a time.
Capture outposts for funds, build militia and cavalry, catapults and hawk riders, and push
toward the enemy headquarters before they reach yours.

It is made in Rust with the Bevy engine, and it runs the same game everywhere: in the
browser (WebAssembly) or as a desktop build for Windows, macOS and Linux.

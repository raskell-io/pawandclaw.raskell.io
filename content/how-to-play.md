## Your first battle

1. Open **[Play](/play/)** and pick **Skirmish**.
2. Choose your army, a commander and a battlefield, then **Begin battle**.
3. On your turn, select a unit, pick a tile to move to, then choose what to do there:
   attack, capture, wait, load into a transport, and so on.
4. Select a **workshop**, **dock** or **roost** you own to build new units with your funds.
5. Open the **map menu** and choose **End turn** when you are done.

You win by **capturing the enemy headquarters** or by **routing every enemy unit**.

## The rules of the woods

Paw & Claw follows the rules of *Advance Wars 2* and *Dual Strike*. If you know those, you
know this. The short version:

- **Funds.** Every property you own pays income at the start of your turn. Spend it at
  workshops (ground units), docks (boats) and roosts (fliers).
- **Capturing.** Foot soldiers capture properties. A capture takes 20 points, and each turn
  removes the capturing unit's health (1–10). Leave the tile and the capture resets.
- **Combat.** Damage depends on the attacker, the defender, both units' health and the
  defender's terrain. A unit that survives strikes back if it can reach. Catapults,
  trebuchets and other ranged units can't move and fire in the same turn, but nothing hits
  back at them.
- **Terrain.** Thickets, brambles, boulders and properties give cover; fliers ignore it.
  Roads are fast, creeks are slow, ponds are for boats.
- **Supply.** Units on your own properties heal 2 health and resupply at the start of your
  turn. Fliers and boats burn stamina every day and are lost when it runs out.
- **Fog of war.** On foggy maps you only see what your units see. Thickets and lily pads hide
  units from all but their neighbours.
- **Commanders.** The power meter charges as units are damaged. See the
  [commanders](/commanders/) for what each power does.

## On phones and tablets

Hold the device sideways. Tap to select and act, drag to pan, pinch to zoom, and use the
menu button in the bottom-right corner for the map menu. On an iPhone or iPad you can add
the game to your home screen from Safari's share menu to play full screen.

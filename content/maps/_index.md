The first nine maps are the classic **War Room** maps of *Advance Wars*, copied tile for tile
and translated to the forest floor: sea becomes pond, woods become thickets, mountains become
boulders and cities become storehouses. The rest were made for Paw & Claw. You pick the
armies when you start a skirmish; the previews show the first army in orange and the second
in blue. Or paint your own in the **map editor**.

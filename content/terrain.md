Terrain decides how far units get and how much damage they take. Each **defense star** under
a defender cuts the damage it takes: by 10% per star at full health (40% on four stars), less
as the defender loses health. Fliers get no stars. **Properties** can be captured by foot soldiers;
each one you own pays funds at the start of your turn and repairs and resupplies the units
that sit on it.

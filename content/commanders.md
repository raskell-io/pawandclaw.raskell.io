Armies are cosmetic: units cost and fight the same in every colour. **Commanders** are where
the differences live. Each has abilities that are always on, a **CO Power** and a stronger
**Super CO Power**. The power meter fills as your units take and deal damage; spend it with
three stars, or save up for the super. Either power also gives every unit +10 attack and
defense while it lasts.

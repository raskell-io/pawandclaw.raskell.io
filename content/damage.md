Rows attack, columns defend. Each number is the **base damage** in percent: what a full-health
attacker does to a full-health target with no terrain cover, before commanders and luck. A
value over 100 can destroy a unit in one hit. Where a unit has two weapons the stronger is
shown; hover a cell to see both. An empty cell means that attacker can't hit that target.
The [rules](/rules/#combat) explain how health, terrain and commanders change the result.
